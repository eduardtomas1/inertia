VENDORED THINKING ORBS ENGINE

The optional "Working indicator" orbs draw geometry from the thinking-orbs
engine by Jakub Antalik. The pure geometry modules are vendored in
src/renderer/src/vendor/thinking-orbs; comments and the canvas painters were
removed and nothing else in the math was changed. Source URLs, the pinned
revision, and file hashes are recorded in
src/renderer/src/vendor/thinking-orbs/README.md.
